# Apex Unfollowers for Instagram

## Short description

See who unfollowed you or doesn’t follow you back on Instagram. Compare follower lists and track changes locally.

## Full description

Find out who doesn’t follow you back on Instagram, who you don’t follow back and which accounts follow you mutually. Run a scan to compare your lists. When you scan again later, Apex Unfollowers shows who stopped following you and who started since your previous scan.

• Browse your results with search, sorting and filters. Hide accounts from the non-followers list and export the current tab’s filtered results as a CSV.
• Track follower counts over time with a chart, scan history and summaries for the past 7 and 30 days.
• Unfollow one account at a time. Each action requires confirmation, with a pause between actions and a daily limit.
• Save or restore a local JSON backup of your scan results, history and settings.

Sign in to instagram.com as usual and open Apex Unfollowers. The extension asks for no additional account or password. Scan results, history and settings are stored in your browser. There is no telemetry, and the source code is publicly available on GitHub.

Apex Unfollowers is independent of Instagram and Meta. It uses your existing Instagram web session and works on demand while the Instagram tab is open.
