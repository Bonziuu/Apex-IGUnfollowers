**Full description**

```text
Apex Unfollowers compares your Instagram followers and following lists and shows who doesn't follow you back, who you don't follow and who unfollowed you since your last check.

Key features:
• Don't follow back, You don't follow, Mutual – with search, filters (verified, private, public, no profile photo) and sorting.
• Changes: who left and who joined since the previous scan.
• Statistics: counters, 7 and 30-day balance, follower chart over time.
• Whitelist for accounts you don't expect to follow you (e.g. brands and celebrities).
• Unfollow a single account: with confirmation, breaks between actions and a daily limit.
• Resumable scans for large accounts with an estimated time left.
• CSV export and a backup file for settings and history.
• English and Polish interface, light and dark theme.

No password, no account
The extension uses the session you are already logged into on instagram.com. It never asks for your login or password and needs no registration.

Runs locally
Scan results, history and settings stay in the extension's storage in your browser. The extension connects only to instagram.com, has no server of its own and sends no telemetry to the author. The code is open source (MIT licence).

Careful with your account
Requests are sent slowly, with random breaks, and the extension waits when Instagram asks to slow down. Unfollowing is manual only, one account at a time. Instagram does not publish its limits – on first run the extension explains how to use it safely.

Apex Unfollowers is an independent project, not affiliated with Instagram or Meta Platforms. Instagram is a trademark of Meta Platforms, Inc.
```

**Single purpose**

```text
Showing the user the follow relationships of their own Instagram account (who doesn't follow them back and who unfollowed them), with the option to manually unfollow selected accounts.
```
